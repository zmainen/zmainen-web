Zach Mainen

Lisbon, Portugal

I am a Principal Investigator at the Champalimaud Centre for the Unknown in Lisbon, where I run the Mainen Lab studying serotonin, decision-making, and perception. I co-founded the International Brain Laboratory, a collaboration rethinking how neuroscience is done. I also write on artificial intelligence, formal ontology, and the design of institutions, and founded Latent States, a research programme exploring subjective experience through science and art.

Research

Mainen Lab
mainenlab.org
Systems neuroscience of serotonin, decision-making, and rapid perception. Neural circuits underlying confidence, uncertainty, and flexible behavior in mice and humans.
Inscription
arXiv:2603.21272
A multi-paper research program on how the organization of external memory determines reasoning capacity. Spans computational complexity, formal ontology, multi-agent systems, and AI governance.
Topological Theory of Things
topological.eu
A relational, pluralistic ontology — from phenomenology to data structures. Flat, formally precise, and now applied to designing ontologies for general world models.
Latent States
latentstates.org
Situated experiments at the intersection of neuroscience, art, and subjective experience. Festival research, immersive installations, and collaborative work across perception, consciousness, and embodied cognition.
